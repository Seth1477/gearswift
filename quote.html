What to send

  • Have a drawing? Send it, along with the CAD model if you have one.
  • Only have the part? Photograph it next to a ruler and give us the OD, bore, face width and tooth count.
  • Know the tooth data? Module or DP, pressure angle and helix angle save a lot of back-and-forth.
  • Unsure of the material? Describe the application and we'll recommend one.
