Spur gears made to your spec

Spur gears have straight teeth cut parallel to the shaft. They are simple, efficient and the most common gear in machinery, from pumps and conveyors to transmissions, winches and gear reducers.

We cut spur gears in metric module or diametral pitch, with whatever bore your shaft needs: plain, keyed, splined, or with a bolt pattern for a hub. Gear steels include 4140, 4340, 8620 and 9310, carburized and ground or through-hardened, and other materials on request.

From one replacement gear to a production run

Need one gear to get a machine running? Send the old one, or photos with the outside diameter, tooth count, face width and bore. Building new equipment? Send the drawing and we will review it before anything gets cut.

Once your gear is on file, reorders are faster because the tooling and CNC programs are already set up. Every order is inspected and can ship with a certificate of conformance, material certs and an inspection report.

Questions

Do you cut metric and inch spur gears?

Yes. We make gears in metric module and in diametral pitch, and can tell which one your old gear is when we measure it.

Can you put a spline or keyway in the bore?

Yes. Internal splines, keyways, set screws and bolt patterns are all common, and we match them to your shaft or hub.

How fast can you build a gear?

It depends on the size, the material and whether the gear needs heat treat and grinding. Send photos and we will come back with a firm ship date. Repeat orders are faster because your tooling and programs stay on file, and expedite options are available when equipment is down.

Do I need a drawing?

No. A drawing is best, but clear photos of the old gear next to a ruler, plus the outside diameter, bore, face width and tooth count, are often enough. We reverse engineer the gear from the part.

What kinds of gears do you make?

Industrial gears: spur, helical and double-helical (herringbone) gears, planetary sun, planet and ring gears, pump jack reducer gearing, internal gears, worms and threaded shafts, splined gears and shafts, sprockets, and pinion shafts. We do not make automotive gears. If it transmits motion in industrial equipment, send it over and we will tell you if we can make it.

Can you make just one?

Yes. Single replacement gears and prototypes are welcome, and so are production runs and repeat orders.

Will I get paperwork with the gear?

Every gear is inspected before it ships, and documentation is available with your order: certificate of conformance, material certs, heat treat certs and an inspection report.
