Questions

How fast can you build a gear?

It depends on the size, the material and whether the gear needs heat treat and grinding. Send photos and we will come back with a firm ship date. Repeat orders are faster because your tooling and programs stay on file, and expedite options are available when equipment is down.

Do I need a drawing?

No. A drawing is best, but clear photos of the old gear next to a ruler, plus the outside diameter, bore, face width and tooth count, are often enough. We reverse engineer the gear from the part.

What kinds of gears do you make?

Spur, helical and double-helical (herringbone) gears, internal gears, worms and threaded shafts, splined gears and shafts, sprockets, and pinion shafts. If it transmits motion, send it over and we will tell you if we can make it.

Can you make just one?

Yes. Single replacement gears and prototypes are welcome, and so are production runs and repeat orders.

Will I get paperwork with the gear?

Every gear is inspected before it ships, and documentation is available with your order: certificate of conformance, material certs, heat treat certs and an inspection report.
