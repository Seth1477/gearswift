Replace the worn gearing, not the whole reducer

Most pump jack gear reducers use double-reduction herringbone gearing: a high-speed pinion, an intermediate gear and pinion, and a large bull gear driving the crank. When the teeth pit, spall or break, the housing, shafts and bull gear are often still good. Replacing just the worn gearing gets the unit back in service for a fraction of the cost of a new reducer.

We measure your worn pinion or gear, check the mating gear, and build a replacement that meshes with what you keep. No drawing needed. Send photos next to a tape measure with the outside diameter, face width and tooth count, plus the nameplate rating if you have it.

Questions

Can you replace just the pinion?

Yes. The high-speed pinion usually wears first. We measure the mating gear so the new pinion matches its tooth geometry and centre distance. If the gear is worn too, we will tell you before you order.

What do I need to send for a quote?

Photos of the worn gear or pinion next to a tape measure, the outside diameter, face width and tooth count, and the reducer nameplate if you have it. No drawing needed.

How fast can you build it?

It depends on the size and heat treatment. Every quote comes with a firm ship date, and expedite options are available when a unit is down.

Do you rebuild the whole gear reducer?

We make the gears and pinion shafts. Your gearbox repair shop can install them, and we are happy to work directly with them.

Do you ship outside Alberta?

Yes. We ship pump jack gears to Saskatchewan, Manitoba, British Columbia and anywhere else in Canada.

Do you make car or truck gears?

No. We make industrial gears only, such as pump jack reducer gearing. We do not make automotive transmission or differential gears.
